# Inspect notification-triggered execution history

## Read an event's triggering records

1. Open Notifications.
2. On desktop, click the notification's Execution history icon. On mobile, choose Trigger history from its overflow menu.
3. Read the trigger time, recorded execution condition and target identifiers.
4. Open the target chat or Flow history separately to check its actual result.

## Completion and recovery

Retry a failed history read. An empty list only establishes that no records were returned for this notification. These cards do not report final outcomes or provide result-navigation controls. There is no combined Routine execution-history screen to use for every trigger type.
