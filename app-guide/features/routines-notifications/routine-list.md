# Manage automatic Routines

## Create a Routine

1. Open Routine on desktop or Shortcuts → Routine on mobile.
2. Choose Create and one of DingDong notification, Mobile notification or Scheduler.
3. Choose AI response or Flow.
4. For AI response, select a room and your responding DingDong. For Flow, select the target Flows in its editor.
5. Configure the trigger and save. On desktop, review the creation summary before the final save.

## Maintain rules

Search or filter by trigger, target and activation state. Open a rule to inspect its configuration, edit it, toggle Enabled or delete it with confirmation. Rules reached through DingDong or Flow settings are the same automation rules managed here.

## Check what happened

Refresh after a load or change error. Use notification trigger history for a particular DingDong notification and Flow history for a particular Flow's runs. Pausing a Routine does not cancel work already started.
