# Read and manage notifications

## Read a notification

1. Open the upper-right notification icon on desktop or Home → Notifications on mobile.
2. Select an entry to mark it read and open its supported destination.
3. To inspect event triggering, use the desktop Execution history icon or mobile overflow → Trigger history.
4. Open the target chat or Flow history separately to inspect the result.

## Maintain the inbox

Use Mark all read to clear unread DingDong notifications. Use Delete on an individual notification and confirm removal. Load more entries or refresh/retry when data is incomplete. Mobile also lists service announcements; opening them can show details or a linked action.

## Completion and recovery

A failed-open message means the destination did not open successfully. A notification's read state and the completion of its triggered work are separate. Deleting an inbox item does not disable its Hook or cancel work.
