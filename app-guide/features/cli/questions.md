# Answer DingDong questions in the terminal

Use the bottom question panel and review all answers before submission.

## Start here

**Desktop:** A waiting DingDong opens the bottom questions panel; /answer reopens it.

**Mobile:** Use mobile’s DingDong question sheet instead of terminal keys.

## Desktop steps

1. When questions appear, choose suggestions with Up/Down or type your answer.
2. Use Left/Right or Tab/Shift+Tab to move through the set.
3. Press Enter to confirm each answer.
4. Review the whole set, then Enter to submit.
5. Use /answer to reopen a hidden panel or /stop to end the waiting run.

## Mobile steps

1. Open DingDong needs your answer in the mobile room.
2. Answer and review the set with the touch controls.
3. Use the desktop TUI for the listed keyboard workflow.

## CLI steps

The chat draft is retained while answering questions. After submission the transcript shows the question/answer set.

## Completion, limits and recovery

Esc does not discard answers or stop the waiting AI by itself. Failed submissions preserve the answers for retry. Switching target/thread or ending the login session can invalidate the pending question set.
