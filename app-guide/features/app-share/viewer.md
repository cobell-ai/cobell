# Open a local or shared app

## Open a chat share

1. Use normal sign-in and open the chat where the app is shared.
2. Select the Apps grid icon in the chat header. Refresh if necessary, then choose the app. You can also open Apps from desktop navigation or mobile Shortcuts and select a room share.
3. Use the app in its viewer. Access depends on current chat participation, the operator's settings and app/plan availability.
4. To keep the connection while returning to Cobell, use desktop Minimize or mobile Back. Restore from the floating desktop chip or mobile Apps → Open connections.
5. To end the connection, use desktop Close or mobile Close connection.

App viewing on iOS requires iOS 17 or later. The embedded macOS viewer requires macOS 14 or later. App data is stored per chat. On desktop, **Open in Chrome** uses separate saved data from the built-in viewer.

A temporary pause or hold can retain the page while access is suspended. If the status says Connection ended or the host changed, open the app again to establish a new connection. Check the displayed reason before retrying. Agent sign-in cannot view shared apps, including its own locally hosted apps.

## Open your own folder on this device

Sign in normally and select your app in Apps. If its KnowledgeBox folder is linked to this device, choose **Run**. This works before enabling a chat share. **Reset app data** in the app details clears its saved data on this device and keeps the source files.
