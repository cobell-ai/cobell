# Resume a desktop checkout

Platform Plan, AI Tier and External AI Slots are independent subscriptions with separate charges and billing dates. Each product is managed on its purchase platform; other products may use different platforms. Changing or ending one product leaves the others unchanged. Organization remains unavailable.

## Start here

**Desktop:** Profile, Subscription Change or Payment Management can show Previous checkout. An active checkout opens Waiting for payment and a browser.

**Mobile:** Use the separate mobile-store restoration flow.

## Recover the existing attempt

1. Open Profile or Payment Management and find Previous checkout.
2. Read its status and reviewed amount. Retry a failed status read.
3. Choose Continue checkout to resume/recheck the saved attempt.
4. If the browser did not open, choose Open checkout again in Waiting for payment.
5. Wait for Cobell to confirm completion, failure or expiry. Inspect the subscription and history before considering another purchase.
6. For a card-registration-only reminder, Hide reminder removes the reminder without promising cancellation at the provider.

## Conditions and recovery

Opening a browser or receiving a payment-return link only prompts a status check; it is not proof of success. The app waits for confirmed payment status. Closing a dialog, a local timeout and hiding a reminder do not undo a provider transaction.

An unresolved paid checkout can block another subscription change. Resume the saved attempt rather than opening a duplicate. Card-registration recovery is separate from purchasing subscription capacity.
