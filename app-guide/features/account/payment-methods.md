# Manage desktop payment methods

Platform Plan, AI Tier and External AI Slots are independent subscriptions with separate charges and billing dates. Each product is managed on its purchase platform; other products may use different platforms. Changing or ending one product leaves the others unchanged. Organization remains unavailable.

## Start here

**Desktop:** Profile → Payment Management → Payment Method. Scheduled changes use the Scheduled tab.

**Mobile:** Use Manage subscription in the mobile store panel where offered. Desktop saved-card editing is separate from store management.

## Payment methods

1. Open Payment Method and inspect the current provider and saved cards.
2. On the Korea card-registration route, choose the offered country and Add new card; complete its browser registration.
3. For Paddle, use Payment Management to open the customer portal. Standalone Add card is unavailable for that route; paid checkout registers its payment method.
4. Choose Set as default for another saved card if required.
5. To remove a card, choose Delete card, read its renewal impact and confirm; Cancel keeps it.

## Scheduled changes

Open Scheduled to read the effective date. Cancel scheduled change requires confirmation. The confirmation's Cancel leaves the pending change in place. Check the result after returning to Profile.

## Conditions and recovery

Deleting a card disables automatic renewals that use it. It does not itself request a refund. Add-card controls can be blocked by a previous checkout or unknown checkout state.

Apple/Google subscriptions show a platform notice in desktop payment-method and scheduled-change tabs. Manage those subscriptions on their original platform. Failed operations display an error; do not assume a changed default, removed card or cancelled schedule until the result is confirmed.
