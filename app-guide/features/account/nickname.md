# Change your nickname

Change the display name shown to other Cobell users.

## Start here

**Desktop:** Click your avatar/name near the upper-right of the main window. In User Info, click the pencil beside your current nickname.

**Mobile:** Tap the Home profile header or More profile card, then Account settings.

## Desktop steps

1. Click your avatar/name in the upper-right profile area.
2. Find User Info and click the pencil immediately beside your name.
3. In Edit nickname, enter a different valid Nickname.
4. Click Save; the dialog closes after a successful update.

## Mobile steps

1. Tap the fifth bottom tab, More.
2. Tap the profile card, then Account settings.
3. Edit Name.
4. Tap the bottom Save button and confirm the updated name on Profile.

## Completion, limits and recovery

Names use 1–40 supported letters, digits or underscores, without spaces, and cannot be digits only. Leading/trailing whitespace is trimmed. Save is unavailable for an unchanged or invalid name and while saving. A failed update leaves the editor available for retry; mobile keeps the draft. Ordinary account editing requires your personal login.
