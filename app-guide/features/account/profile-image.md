# Change your profile image

Choose a local image, generate an AI candidate, or remove your current avatar.

## Start here

**Desktop:** Click your top-right avatar/name, then the camera control on the profile image in User Info.

**Mobile:** Home profile header or More → Profile → Change Profile Image; also available in Account settings.

## Desktop steps

1. Open Profile and click the camera control.
2. Choose image from device to upload, or Generate profile image with AI.
3. For generation, enter optional guidance and wait.
4. Review the candidate and choose Apply, or Regenerate.
5. To remove the avatar, choose Delete profile image and confirm.

## Mobile steps

1. Open More → profile card → Change Profile Image.
2. Choose a device image, or Generate profile image with AI.
3. For generation, optionally choose a reference, style and prompt, then Generate.
4. Review and Apply; Generate again or Discard keeps the current avatar.
5. For removal, choose Delete profile image and confirm.

## Completion, limits and recovery

Generation and applying are separate actions. The optional generation prompt accepts 80 characters. Mobile accepts nonempty JPEG, PNG or WebP files within 10 MiB for profile images and references. Removing a reference clears the input, not the applied avatar.

Mobile's generation status card can reopen progress or a candidate. Cancel stops generation, Retry repeats a failed request, and Discard removes an unaccepted candidate. Desktop Close leaves generation running; reopen its status from the profile image controls. Resolve any failed apply before treating the avatar as updated.
