# Review or change a subscription

Platform Plan, AI Tier and External AI Slots are independent subscriptions with separate charges and billing dates. Each product is managed on its purchase platform; other products may use different platforms. Changing or ending one product leaves the others unchanged. Organization remains unavailable.

## Start here

**Desktop:** Upper-right avatar/name → Profile → Subscription & Payment. The Plan, AI Tier and External AI Credential Slots sections offer Change Subscription or a subscription/upgrade button.

**Mobile:** Home profile header or More profile card → Profile → Subscription & Payment.

## Desktop steps

1. Open the subscription section and choose Change Subscription, Subscribe to Plan or Upgrade Tier.
2. Select the offered billing country if required, then plan, AI tier and paid-slot capacities.
3. Wait for a valid quote, then choose Next.
4. Review itemized charges, timing and any affected-slot warning. Previous returns to selection; Cancel leaves without submitting this change.
5. Submit the reviewed change. If checkout opens in a browser, wait for Cobell's confirmed result.
6. Check the updated subscription or scheduled change. Use the previous-checkout controls if the result is unresolved.

## Mobile steps

1. Open Subscription & Payment and read the current status and provider.
2. Use the Mobile subscription panel for offered store products, purchase restoration or store management.
3. Open AI usage, Payment history or External AI slot entitlements for detail.
4. Refresh or pull down to recheck changes after returning from the store.

## Cancellation and expiry

Cancelling renewal for an active paid subscription preserves access through its current paid period. The account returns to Free when the subscription fully ends. Apple/Google renewals are managed in the original store.

Cobell-provided access ends on its displayed end date without automatic renewal or payment. The account then returns to Free. A paid subscription requires a separate purchase after the provided access has ended; an earlier paid subscription does not restart automatically. Subscription configuration changes are unavailable while provided access is active.

## Expiry precautions

| Item | Effect when access ends | Using it again |
|---|---|---|
| Device registrations above the Free limit | Excess registrations are removed and their normal/agent sessions end. Registrations within the limit remain. | Sign in again to register within the available limit. A new subscription does not restore removed registrations or sessions. |
| External AI slots whose entitlement ends | Stored credentials and bound settings are deleted from Cobell. They are not retained as disabled slots. Separately granted slots remain while their own entitlement is valid. | New capacity requires credential registration and slot configuration again. Deleted credentials and settings do not return after payment. |
| Agent sign-in tokens above the active limit | Tokens are plan-paused and their associated agent sessions end. | An eligible token can be reactivated when capacity is available; increasing the plan does not reactivate it automatically. |

Before expiry, explain these effects to the user. See [device registration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cobell-ai/cobell/main/app-guide/features/devices-browsers/device-management.md), [slot entitlements](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cobell-ai/cobell/main/app-guide/features/account/credential-capacity.md) and [agent tokens](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cobell-ai/cobell/main/app-guide/features/account/agent-tokens.md). A later purchase does not undo completed removals.

## Conditions and recovery

Manage an existing subscription on its purchase platform. Desktop shows a platform notice for Apple/Google subscriptions that have not fully ended. Turning off renewal or pausing alone does not make a different platform available.

During payment-failure/grace or paused states, configuration changes can be blocked; read the payment notice and management action. A checkout timeout, browser return or store purchase launch is not proof of a completed subscription change. Resolve the existing attempt before starting another.
