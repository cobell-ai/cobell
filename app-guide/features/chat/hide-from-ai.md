# Exclude messages from AI context

Keep a message visible to people while controlling whether DingDong can read it.

## Start here

**Desktop:** Use the composer Hide mode, or a message’s More/right-click → Hide or Unhide.

**Mobile:** Use composer Hidden, or message More → Hide from AI / Show to AI.

## Desktop steps

1. Before sending, enable Hide if the message should stay out of AI context.
2. For an existing message, use its More menu → Hide.
3. To restore inclusion, choose Unhide when the message and room allow it.

## Mobile steps

1. Enable Hidden in the composer for an AI-excluded message.
2. For existing content, use More → Hide from AI.
3. Use Show to AI when available to reverse that exclusion. You can also tap AI cannot see this message and choose Show to AI in the explanation.
4. If the explanation shows a disabled Cannot show to AI button, read the reason and use Close to return.

## Completion, limits and recovery

Hiding from AI does not remove the message from participants. Recipient-filtered and some special messages cannot be included in AI context. Composer hiding and thread creation cannot be enabled together.

On mobile, recipient-filtered messages, P2P file offers, DingDong notifications and bot termination messages are always excluded. They show the AI exclusion indicator even when Hidden was not manually selected. Their More menu has no hide/show action, and the explanation cannot restore AI inclusion.

Restoring an ordinary hidden message requires a confirmed sent message, a writable view, room send permission and support for message changes. Pending, failed and read-only messages cannot be restored through this control.
