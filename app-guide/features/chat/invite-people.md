# Invite people and approve entry votes

Add regular or guest participants and handle invitation approvals.

## Start here

**Desktop:** Chat panel → right-click the room row → Chat Room Settings → Invite People.

**Mobile:** Second bottom tab Chat → open the room → gear → Invite.

## Desktop steps

1. Open Chat Room Settings → Invite People.
2. Select people and set Regular or Guest participation.
3. Click Invite.
4. Use Vote to review pending entry requests and Approve or Reject.

## Mobile steps

1. Open room settings → Invite.
2. Select people and their Participation type.
3. Submit Invite.
4. Use Votes for pending approval decisions.

## Completion, limits and recovery

Guests see messages sent after they joined and cannot invite their own DingDongs. A guest with Bell access can still ask a participating DingDong about earlier AI-visible context. Managers-only invitation settings and blocked-user/DingDong-owner conflicts can prevent completion.

If a mobile group was created but starting its invitations failed, use Retry in the room's failure banner. It resends the stored selection for that room and shows progress. Failure retains the banner; creating another room is not required. Check participants and approval votes after an uncertain result.
