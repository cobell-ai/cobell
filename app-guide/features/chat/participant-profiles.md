# Read human participant profiles

Inspect a person’s relationship, available chats and room-specific actions.

## Start here

**Desktop:** Click the person’s avatar in the friend list, room header or message. Clicking your own avatar inside a room opens your own profile dialog.

**Mobile:** Tap the person’s avatar in a friend/participant list or message.

## Desktop steps

1. Click the person’s avatar.
2. Review their relationship and accessible Chat Rooms.
3. Open an existing room, or use Create New Room/New Group Chat when offered.
4. Use Send Friend Request if needed.
5. For your own in-room profile, review My DingDongs in this chat or Invite DingDong.

## Mobile steps

1. Tap the person’s avatar in the room or directory.
2. Read the relationship and available room actions.
3. Open a related chat or start an eligible new one.
4. Use friendship/safety or role actions only after reviewing their impact.

## Completion, limits and recovery

The profile’s actions depend on the current account, relationship, room and role. A user without an explicit friendship cannot start a new friend direct room. Blocking, unfriending and kicking are different actions. Your global Profile page differs from the compact in-room own-profile dialog.

Also find this under: human profile, friend avatar, own profile dialog, related chats.
