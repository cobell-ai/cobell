# Manage representatives, managers and kicked users

Manage participation roles in a group you are authorized to administer.

## Start here

**Desktop:** Chat Room Settings → Room Management; participant profiles also expose permitted role actions.

**Mobile:** Room settings → Roles → Representative, Managers or Kicked Users.

## Desktop steps

1. Open Chat Room Settings → Room Management.
2. Select the representative or manager control and choose the person.
3. Review and confirm the role change.
4. Use participant profiles for permitted Kick, Appoint or Dismiss actions.
5. Open Kicked List and Unkick when re-entry should be allowed.

## Mobile steps

1. Open room settings → Roles.
2. Use Representative to choose a successor, or Managers to adjust the manager selection.
3. Save/confirm the offered action.
4. Use Kicked Users → Restore for permitted re-entry.

## Completion, limits and recovery

Role changes require the corresponding authority. A representative may need to appoint another person before leaving. Removing a human owner also affects their invited DingDongs; previous messages are not automatically deleted.

Also find this under: host, representative, manager, kick, restore user, invite restrictions.
