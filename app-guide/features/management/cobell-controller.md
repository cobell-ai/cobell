# Control the Cobell app with Cobelly

Cobell Controller is the built-in Toolbox for controlling the Cobell app and using its features. Ask Cobelly to perform supported app actions on your behalf. The available tools determine which actions are currently supported.

## Start here

**Desktop:** Start/System DingDong prompt, or a Memory/Gear creation dialog’s Create through conversation option.

**Mobile:** Home’s System DingDong prompt, or Create with System DingDong in configuration creation.

## Desktop steps

1. Open Start and describe what you want to do in Cobell.
2. For assisted creation, you can also choose Create through conversation during Memory or Gear creation.
3. Answer any questions Cobelly needs to perform the action.
4. Review the reported result and check the relevant app screen. Complete any remaining steps that require your direct action.

## Mobile steps

1. Open Home’s System DingDong prompt and describe what you want to do in Cobell.
2. For assisted creation, you can also use the Memory/Toolbox/Gear creation action.
3. Answer any follow-up questions.
4. Review the reported result and check the relevant app screen. Complete any remaining steps that require your direct action.

## Completion, limits and recovery

Cobell Controller is automatically included for System DingDong Gear. Its catalog is a brief capability overview, not published hidden tool schemas. A saved object, a valid configuration and successful execution are separate completion stages.

Actions that use a device still require that device and its applicable permissions.
