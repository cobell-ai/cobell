# Finish account registration

Supply the additional information needed when signing in for the first time.

## Start here

**Desktop:** The provider sign-in opens Sign Up when the account is not registered.

**Mobile:** The provider flow opens Create account when registration is needed.

## Desktop steps

1. Sign in with Google or Apple.
2. In Sign Up, enter Nickname and review the email.
3. Read and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
4. Click Sign Up.

## Mobile steps

1. Start the Google or Apple sign-in flow.
2. Enter Name on Create account and review the displayed email.
3. Accept the required agreements.
4. Submit registration.

## Completion, limits and recovery

The name must use 1–40 supported letters, numbers or underscores and cannot be numbers only. An expired signup requires a fresh social sign-in. If the account is already registered, return to login.

Also find this under: signup, create account, first login, nickname required.
