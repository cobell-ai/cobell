# Unfriend, block or unblock a person: screen reference

Choose the relationship action whose chat consequences you intend.

## Open this screen

**Desktop:** Click the human avatar to open their profile; a bot profile also offers Block Owner.

**Mobile:** Open the participant’s human profile or a bot’s owner safety control.

## Controls

| Control or area | Location and behavior |
|---|---|
| Unfriend | Removes explicit friendship but leaves existing chats active. |
| Block / Block Owner | Blocks the human account and affects that owner’s bots. |
| Keep previous messages | Hides only later messages while blocked. |
| Delete previous messages | Applies a visibility filter to existing messages too. |
| Rooms to check | Lists shared rooms containing your DingDongs that must be resolved first. |
| Unblock | Removes the block; does not reopen closed direct chats. |
| Confirm / Cancel | Applies or abandons the reviewed action. |

## Conditions and results

Unfriending does not stop existing conversations, but it prevents new friend direct rooms until friendship is restored. Blocking permanently closes existing direct chats; unblocking does not reopen them. Group chats continue, and hidden messages sent during a block can reappear after unblocking. The previous-message option is a display filter, not physical message deletion.
