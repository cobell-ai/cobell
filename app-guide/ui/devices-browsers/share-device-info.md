# Share device information with your own devices: screen reference

Control which information this device sends to your other connected devices.

## Open this screen

**Desktop:** Profile → Share Information Between My Devices; Management → Device also has Share Info.

**Mobile:** More → profile card → Share Information Between My Devices, or the device detail share switch.

## Controls

| Control or area | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Share this device's information with my other devices | Saves outgoing sharing immediately; there is no separate Save button. |
| Shared information and usage | Desktop lists OS type/version, computer name, OS account name and model. Mobile lists OS type/version and model; fields depend on the device. |
| Retry | Mobile offers Retry after a settings load/save failure; it reloads the current setting before another change. |
| Close / Back | Leaves the explanation. Management's share switch changes the device in that row/detail; the Profile page concerns this device. |

## Conditions and recovery

Information is sent directly to your connected devices over encrypted P2P, not to other people or Cobell. Turning outgoing sharing off does not stop receiving information shared by your other devices. Missing machine details can reflect sharing or connectivity; they are separate from device registration and tool permissions.
