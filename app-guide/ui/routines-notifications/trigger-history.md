# Inspect notification-triggered execution history: screen reference

Read triggering records for a specific DingDong notification.

## Open this screen

**Desktop:** Notifications → the notification's Execution history icon.

**Mobile:** Home → Notifications → notification overflow menu → Trigger history.

## Controls

| Control or area | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Recorded entries | Shows trigger time, recorded normal-session condition and responder/room identifiers where available. |
| Retry | Reloads a failed history read. |
| Close / Back | Leaves history. |

## Conditions and results

The cards are records, not links to edit a rule or open a result. They do not display final success/failure of the AI response or Flow. Open the destination chat or the Flow's own execution history separately to inspect completion.

An empty list means no record was returned for this notification. It is not combined history for all Schedulers or Android events. Check the target chat or Flow for execution results.
